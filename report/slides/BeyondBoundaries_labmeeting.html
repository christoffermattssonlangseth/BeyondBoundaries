What does the Xenium multimodal segmentation kit tell us that the transcriptome doesn't?

Mouse EAE spinal cord, RRMAP2 runs 5 + 6 · 18 Xenium 5K images · 51 tissue pieces · 25 animals · 500,379 annotated cells · 201 image features per cell

01
The question

Four stains are imaged anyway, to draw the cell masks. Is there biology in them?

DAPI · ATP1A1/CD45/E-Cad (boundary) · 18S rRNA (interior; drew ~95 % of masks) · αSMA/Vimentin. Per cell: intensity in nucleus / cytoplasm / rim / surrounding ring, texture, polarity, shape. Vim, Acta2 and Atp1a1 are not on the 5K panel.

Bottom line

Three kinds of added information. No hidden cell states.

Protein

…for genes not on the panel

Vimentin, αSMA, ATP1A1 per cell. Astrocyte vimentin lags reactive RNA in active disease.

Tissue

…context around each cell

White-matter neuropil loss in lesions (−25 %). Anatomy that adds to the transcriptome (0.68 → 0.73).

Images alone

…recognise cells and map lesions

Cell type 46 % (chance 7 %), lesion maps AUROC 0.90, and this holds across imaging runs.

Not added: CD45 (undetectable in mouse cord); lesion identity and clinical score beyond what the transcriptome already gives. ~85 % of image variance is unexplained by RNA, but that's mostly optics.

notebook 0304
How much of the image is new?

Most of the image isn't in the transcriptome, but most of that is optics

  • Covariates + 50 transcriptome PCs explain a median ~15 % of each image feature (best: nucleus:cell ratio, rim/cyto intensity).
  • The remainder is spatially coherent, but largely a local field shared across cell types (r ≈ 0.38; follows neuropil glow, not lesions).
  • The cell-specific remainder barely moves with lesion state: 19/924 tests, ~0.1 SD.
  • Residual clustering: no discrete hidden states, only continuous gradients.
The kit is not a source of new cell types. It's a source of new measurements for specific questions.

Biology only the images show · 1

White-matter lesions lose 25 % of the ATP1A1 around each cell

Lesion vs healthy WM within the same piece: 38 pieces, p = 8×10⁻⁶. Grey matter: no change (p = 0.62). This is tissue damage the cell's own transcriptome can't carry. Not related to clinical score.

notebooks 06–0711
Candidate · needs replication

Smoother 18S texture in sicker animals

  • Rises with clinical score in 6 cell types
  • Holds within section (ρ 0.53–0.86) and after a crowding adjustment (0.41–0.81)
  • DAPI texture (same optics) doesn't follow, so it's probably not focus
  • Ribosome redistribution / RNA degradation, or tissue handling per animal

Animal level: score from images ρ 0.62 vs transcriptome 0.79. Images are informative but redundant.

Caveats & next steps

Caveats

  • Lesion niches are transcriptome-defined, so image-vs-RNA comparisons for lesions are tilted toward RNA
  • αSMA and vimentin share a channel
  • 18S drew ~95 % of the masks
  • 25 animals, 2 CFA pieces; 2 runs from one batch

Next

  • Replicate on runs 1–3 (P drive): 18S texture, WM neuropil loss, astrocyte RNA → protein lag, image-model transfer
